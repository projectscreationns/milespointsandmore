What Card Should You Apply for Next?

StrategyCredit Cards
Updated Jul 25, 2026

I get this question all the time. The answer changed in January, so if you're working off an old plan, bin it.

What changed

Chase used to make you wait 48 months and then you could go again on the same Sapphire card. That's gone. Since 22 January 2026 it's one bonus per Sapphire card, per lifetime.

Old rule
Every 48 months
churn the same Sapphire forever
Now
Once. Ever.
per Sapphire card, per lifetime

There is one bit of good news buried in it: Preferred and Reserve count as separate cards now. If you've never taken the bonus on one of them, you can still get it - even while holding the other. That wasn't possible before.

The big shift: your Sapphire bonuses are now one-shot. Don't waste them on a mediocre offer.

The new order of operations

Check 5/24Under 5 personal cards in 24 months, or Chase won't approve you. Credit Karma shows your open dates
Burn your one-shots firstSapphire while you're under 5/24 - and only at a peak offer
Then business cardsThey don't count toward 5/24 and still repeat. This is the engine now
Then everyone elseCiti, Cap One, BofA, Barclays all still allow repeats on their own clocks

Rule 1: don't waste a one-shot

This is the whole game now. CSP is sitting at 100,000 points - its all-time high. If you take that card at 60k because you couldn't wait, you've permanently thrown away 40,000 points. There's no second go.

So: if the offer isn't at or near its record, wait. Check where it's historically peaked before you click apply.

Rule 2: business cards are the engine now

Chase Ink cards don't report to your personal credit file, so they don't eat a 5/24 slot, and they still come back around. That makes them the repeatable part of your portfolio now that Sapphire isn't.

Opening a sole trader / LLC for this is cheap and quick. Stick to miles-and-points bonuses rather than cash and it stays simple.

Which cards repeat, which don't

Card / issuerCan you get the bonus again?
Chase Sapphire PreferredNo - once per lifetime
Chase Sapphire ReserveNo - once per lifetime (separate from CSP)
Amex (any card)No - once per lifetime, always has been
Chase Ink (business)Yes - and doesn't touch 5/24
Chase co-brands (United, Hyatt, IHG)Usually yes, on their own cooldowns
CitiYes - roughly 48 months per family
Capital One / BofA / BarclaysYes - own rules, generally repeatable

So what do you actually apply for next?

If you're under 5/24 and have never had a Sapphire: take CSP at 100k now. It's the record offer and you only get one shot at it.

If you've already used your Sapphire bonuses: stop looking at personal Chase cards for a bit. Go Ink for the repeatable business bonuses, then work through Citi, Cap One and BofA - they'll still have you back.

If you're over 5/24: nothing from Chase will approve. Sit on business cards and other issuers until you drop back under.

Old plan: churn the same few cards forever. New plan: spend your one-shots carefully, then live off business cards and the issuers that still let you repeat.
